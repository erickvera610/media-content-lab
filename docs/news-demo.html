Train on real news data, inspect the prediction

The model was trained in Python on historical UCI/Mashable article metadata. This demo runs inference with the exported model; it does not claim current audience performance.

Articles from the same publication-day group never cross training, selection or test boundaries. Share outcomes, article URLs and observation age are excluded from prediction inputs.

Understand the limitation
The target is log1p(shares). The back-transformed prediction represents a typical-share estimate, not the arithmetic expected number of shares. Original share counts have a long tail; observation windows vary. Metadata availability at publication time has not been independently audited.
This is a historical one-publisher benchmark. A modest improvement over the baseline does not establish causal impact or transfer to another publisher. Editing fields creates an exploratory scenario, not a content recommendation.
